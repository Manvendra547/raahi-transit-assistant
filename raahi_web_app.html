About this prototype

Route data is the real AMCTSL (Agra-Mathura City Transport Service Ltd.) timetable, published at uputd.gov.in. Ask Raahi calls a live FastAPI + ChromaDB backend running at 127.0.0.1:8000 that performs real retrieval over these routes.

Ask Raahi

Ask where you're going in your own words — Raahi calls the RAG backend, which retrieves the matching route from the real Agra timetable and grounds the answer in it.
